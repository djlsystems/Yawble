## 1. Why this agreement exists

Yawble core is published source-available under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, Apache-2.0 future license (SPDX `FSL-1.1-ALv2`). The Company also expects to offer a hosted service and, later, commercial enterprise software.

Code that arrives under the outbound license alone cannot be moved into a commercial product, relicensed, or cleanly sold with the business. This agreement gives the Company those rights. You keep ownership of your contribution. The public receives whatever license the Company attaches when it ships a version.

A Developer Certificate of Origin is not a substitute. Signing off a commit does not grant the license in this agreement.
